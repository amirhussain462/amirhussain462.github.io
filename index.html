About

Engineering for reliability, continuity and practical execution.

I work across airport logistics, material handling systems and industrial automation, combining project execution with hands-on electromechanical troubleshooting and reliability improvement.

My approach is to understand how the complete system is intended to operate before deciding where the problem is. I work from observation and evidence, moving from the known to the unknown while considering electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, automation, safety and operational factors together. When specialist input is required, I work closely with technicians, OEM engineers and suppliers rather than allowing assumptions to drive the solution.

Over more than a decade in live operational environments, I have learned that good engineering is not only about restoring equipment. It is also about protecting operations, identifying recurring causes, documenting what was learned and leaving the system more reliable for the people who operate and maintain it.
